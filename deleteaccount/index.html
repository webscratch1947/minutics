Delete your Minutics account

Minutics · How to remove your account and its data
What gets deleted: your account sign-in, your saved plan/purchase record, and all data stored on this device (activities, tasks, routines, budget entries, settings). This cannot be undone. You can also email info@minutics.com and we will delete your server-side data within 30 days.

Delete account in 3 steps

  1. Open Minutics and sign in, then tap the Settings tab at the bottom right.
  2. Scroll to the top of the Settings page and tap the red Delete account button shown below.
  3. Confirm the warning dialog. Your account and all local data are wiped and you return to the welcome screen.

Where to tap

➤
The Delete account button is at the top of Settings, next to Log out.
Prefer email? Send a deletion request from your account email to info@minutics.com. We verify and delete your server-side data within 30 days. On-device data is only removable from the app itself (or by uninstalling / clearing app data).
