Privacy Policy

Minutics · Last updated 26 November 2026 · Effective 26 November 2026

The short version: Minutics is a local-first time-tracking app. Your activities, tasks, routines, budget entries and doom-scroll counts are stored on your device, not on our servers. We use servers only for three things you explicitly trigger: (1) your account sign-in, (2) paid plans through Google Play, and (3) optional cloud features you switch on yourself — daily Telegram reports and push notifications. We do not sell your data, we do not run ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

This policy explains what Minutics ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Minutics Android app and to our website at minutics.com. By using Minutics you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Minutics is operated by the Minutics team. For privacy questions, data requests, or to report a concern, contact info@minutics.com. We respond to verifiable requests within 30 days.

2. What we collect, and where it lives

We deliberately separate on-device data from server data.

2.1 Data that stays on your device

The following are saved in your device's local storage and are not uploaded to us:

  • Activity names, categories, timers, logged minutes and daily history
  • "Eat the Frog" starred tasks, tasks, notes and your free-plan limits
  • Routine Tracker items and daily checkmarks
  • Budget Tracker entries, EMI/compound calculations, Time Value salary figures and currency choice
  • Time Waste budget minutes, daily limits and alert history
  • Doom-scroll counter totals and per-app counts
  • Productivity Score inputs and your display/goal preferences
  • Your chosen profile picture (stored locally only)

Because this data never leaves your phone, uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it permanently. It does not carry over to another device or another account.

2.2 Account data (stored with our identity provider)

To sign in we use Amazon Web Services Cognito. When you register or log in we handle:

  • Your email address and password. Passwords are never stored by us — Cognito handles authentication using a secure protocol (SRP), so your password is not sent in readable form.
  • A unique user identifier and session tokens used to keep you signed in.
  • A device identifier used for our single-device session rule (the last device you sign in on owns the session). This prevents account sharing and is stored in our database.

We do not require real-name verification, phone numbers, or dates of birth for your account. Any date of birth you enter in the app’s onboarding is used only on-device to compute your "time left" view and is stored locally, not on our servers.

2.3 Server-side app state (linked to your account)

We store a small ledger row so paid features and account state survive a reinstall:

  • Your email and the plan you hold (free / basic / yearly / lifetime), the date the plan started, and a purchase record containing the Google Play order ID, product ID and purchase token. This lets us restore your purchase and verify refunds.
  • Notification state such as the last date a Time Waste alert was sent, so we do not alert you repeatedly for the same crossing.
  • A one-way hash of your password-reset code when you use "Forgot password" — never the code itself — plus the time it was created and how many wrong attempts were made.

2.4 Optional cloud features (only if you enable them)

  • Telegram daily reports. If you connect a Telegram bot, the bot token and chat ID you enter are sent to our server so the daily report can be scheduled and sent even when the app is closed. The report contains your own on-device totals (Productivity Score, activities, routine checkmarks). We do not read your Telegram messages. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, which removes the stored schedule.
  • Push notifications. If you allow notifications, a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token is registered so we can deliver reminders and Time Waste alerts. You can revoke notification permission in Android settings at any time.
  • Knowledge Gram likes and comments. If you like or comment on an infographic, the comment text and a display name you choose are written to our public GitHub content repository so all users can see them. Please do not post personal information in comments — they are not private.
  • Downloaded / shared images. Images you download or share are generated on your device and saved to your device's gallery. We do not retain copies.

2.5 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, call logs, SMS, photo library contents, browsing history, or messages.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Minutics is not directed at children.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: authentication, restoring paid plans, syncing optional cloud features, and remembering your session.
  • To enforce free-plan limits and prevent abuse of sign-ups and purchases.
  • To send the notifications and Telegram reports you configured.
  • To diagnose failures (for example, why a popup or purchase did not go through) using short error strings — never your personal content.

We do not use your data to train advertising models, build profiles, or make automated decisions that affect you legally.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data: kept until you delete it in the app or uninstall. Clearing app data removes it immediately.
  • Account and ledger data: kept while your account is active. Deleting your account via Settings removes the ledger row and marks the account deleted; Cognito deletion is requested at the same time.
  • Optional Telegram data: removed from our server when you disconnect the integration; report markers are kept only for the current day to prevent duplicates.
  • Reset-code hashes: expire after 10 minutes and are cleared after a successful reset.
  • Comments: stored in our public content repository until removed on request.

To request deletion of anything we hold, email info@minutics.com from your account address. We will verify and delete it within 30 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct, delete: request a copy, correction, or deletion of your server-side data by emailing us.
  • Notifications: turn on/off in Android settings at any time.
  • Accessibility / overlay / battery: all optional; revoke in Android settings. Core time tracking still works without them.
  • Telegram reports: disconnect in Settings at any time.
  • Account deletion: Settings → Delete account.
  • Portability: use Settings → Download Data to export your on-device data to a file.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or similar laws, including the right to object to processing or lodge a complaint with your local regulator. We will honour valid requests.

8. Security

Passwords are handled by Cognito with SRP and are never stored in plain text. API endpoints use CORS allowlists, short-lived tokens, one-way hashes for reset codes, and server-side purchase verification with Google. We restrict admin tools behind a separate secret. No system is perfectly secure, but we design the app so the most sensitive data (your daily activities) never leaves your device.

9. Links and third-party services

Minutics may open external links (for example when you share to WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram or Facebook) or fetch infographic images. Those services have their own privacy policies; once you leave or share to them, their rules apply, not ours.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated in the app or on our website before they take effect.
