Real screens, real pages

Every page of Minutics, up close.

These aren't concepts — each shot below is the actual Android app, captured running with a real week of data. Six pages, no cropping, no filters.

01Timer

The home screen. A live countdown to retirement — years, months, days, hours, minutes and seconds — with today's minutes broken down activity by activity underneath.

  • Running-timer strip pinned at the top with a one-tap Stop
  • Today at a Glance: per-activity bars with minutes tracked
  • Eat the Frog card for your three most important tasks today
  • Retirement date and total lifetime minutes under the countdown

02Activity

Where your activities live — start, stop and manage them all from one page.

  • Tap ▶ on any card to start tracking instantly
  • The running activity shows a live elapsed timer
  • Edit or delete any activity in place — or add a new one with an emoji
  • Stat cards for time tracked, blocks today and focus score

03Life Hub

Sixteen tools behind one door. Search, filter by Time, Finance or Productivity, and open anything — calculators, trackers, gram, tasks — in one tap.

  • Featured Time Value Calculator at the top
  • EMI, Compound Interest and other finance tools
  • Knowledge Gram, My Tasks, Routine Trackers
  • Every figure calculated on your device

04Journal

The record. Streak and week strip on top, a dark summary of today, then every day of the month as a row you can expand down to individual time entries.

  • Consistency streak + this-week logged dots
  • Tap a day to expand activity entries and ranges
  • Every entry shows its time range and duration
  • Full view button opens the day's full report

05Full view

One day as one page. A donut split of every minute you tracked, exact percentages per activity, a summary block — and a Share button to send it anywhere.

  • Per-activity time and percentage breakdown
  • Summary: total tracked, categories used, top category
  • Opens from any day row in the Journal
  • Share as an image straight from the app

06Settings

Your knobs and switches. Display currency, goal type, data backup and notifications — every setting in one plain list, with your data staying on the device.

  • Goal type: Retirement, Age Goal, FIRE or Milestone
  • Display currency switch (INR and more)
  • Notifications toggle for reminders and app alerts
  • "Your data stays on this device" — stated right in the app
