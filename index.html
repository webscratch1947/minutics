Core Philosophy

Time is the only resource you can't earn back.

Most productivity apps help you manage tasks. Minutics helps you understand your life. Every feature is built around one uncomfortable truth: the clock is always running, and what you do with today's minutes determines who you become.


The app connects everything — your remaining years, how you spend each day, what that time is worth in money, and whether you're moving toward your goals or away from them.

Features That Hit Different

Built around real behavioral change.

Time Value

Know what your minute is worth.

Enter your salary and work hours. Minutics calculates a ₹/min figure that flows through the entire app — so wasted time shows up as a real money number, not just a guilt feeling.

₹ per minute ₹ per hour ₹ per day 30+ currencies
Productivity Score

A daily score that actually means something.

Every logged category shifts your 0–100 score. Productive minutes add up. Wasted minutes (social media scrolling, entertainment, time waste) subtract harder. No hiding from it.

+0.4 per productive min −0.5 per wasted min Clamped 0–100
Focus Mode

25 minutes. Ambient piano. No excuses.

A focused timer with looping ambient piano music, running as a foreground Android service. It keeps playing even when you leave the app. One tap to stop from the notification.

25-min countdown Background music Notification control
Life Value (Pro)

See your day's financial value — and what you burned.

Combines your time value with today's categorized activities and budget spending. Shows you what your day was worth and how much of it you gave away to waste categories.

Daily value in ₹ Waste cost Spending deducted Pro feature
Daily Score

Your day, broken down honestly.

Minutics doesn't celebrate participation. The productivity score is a real-time reflection of how you're allocating your hours — where you're investing and where you're leaking time.


Time Waste, Social Media scrolling, and Entertainment drag your score down. Work, Study, Fitness push it up. The math is simple. What you do about it is up to you.
